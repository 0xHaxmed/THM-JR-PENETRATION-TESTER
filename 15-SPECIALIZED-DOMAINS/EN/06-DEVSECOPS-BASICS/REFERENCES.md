# Authoritative References: DevSecOps Basics

## 1. Federal Standards & Governance Frameworks
- **NIST Special Publication 800-218: Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) Version 1.1:**
  - URL: `https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-218/final`
  - Recommendations for mitigating software supply chain risks and embedding security across the SDLC.
- **Executive Order 14028: Improving the Nation's Cybersecurity (2021):**
  - Establishes federal baseline requirements for software supply chain security, Software Bills of Materials (SBOM), and automated pipeline testing.
- **OWASP Software Assurance Maturity Model (SAMM v2.0):**
  - Canonical URL: `https://owaspsamm.org/`
  - An open framework to help organizations analyze and improve their software security posture across Governance, Design, Implementation, Verification, and Operations.

---

## 2. Industry Manifestos & Foundational Literature
- **Manifesto for Agile Software Development (2001):**
  - Canonical URL: `https://agilemanifesto.org/`
  - Defines the 4 core values and 12 principles of Agile software engineering.
- **The DevSecOps Manifesto (Shannon Lietz et al.):**
  - Canonical URL: `https://www.devsecops.org/`
  - Defines the core ethos: *"Leaning in over Always Saying No", "Data & Science over Fear, Uncertainty and Doubt", "Open Contribution & Collaboration over Security-Only Silos"*.
- **The DevOps Handbook (Gene Kim, Jez Humble, Patrick Debois, John Willis):**
  - The seminal guide on establishing continuous integration, continuous delivery, and cross-functional organizational trust.
