# Authoritative References: The Blue Team Perspective

## 1. Incident Response Standards & Federal Publications
- **NIST Special Publication 800-61 Revision 2: Computer Security Incident Handling Guide:**
  - URL: `https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-61/rev-2/final`
  - Defines the 4-phase incident response lifecycle (Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment/Eradication/Recovery, Post-Incident Activity) and event taxonomies.
- **SANS Institute: Incident Handler's Handbook:**
  - Authors: Patrick Kral et al.
  - URL: `https://www.sans.org/white-papers/33901/`
  - The foundational reference for the 6-phase SANS PICERL framework.
- **ISO/IEC 27035: Information Security Incident Management:**
  - International standard for establishing an enterprise incident management capability.

---

## 2. Threat Intelligence & Detection Frameworks
- **David J. Bianco: "The Pyramid of Pain" (2013):**
  - Canonical Blog: `https://detect-respond.blogspot.com/2013/03/the-pyramid-of-pain.html`
  - Defines the operational pain levels inflicted on adversaries across Hash Values, IP Addresses, Domain Names, Network/Host Artifacts, Tools, and TTPs.
- **MITRE ATT&CK for Enterprise Matrix:**
  - URL: `https://attack.mitre.org/matrices/enterprise/`
  - The globally accessible knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures based on real-world observations.
- **Mandiant M-Trends Reports:**
  - URL: `https://cloud.google.com/security/m-trends`
  - Authoritative industry metrics on global median dwell time, multi-stage extortion, and external vs. internal notification trends.
